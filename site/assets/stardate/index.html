  1. Stardate 2026.268 25 September 2026

    1. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026, 20:03 Finished

      Deep dream, day one

      Our first full clean-up pass over the main studio's memory retired 3,618 of 117,506 live memories, 3.1%, all reversibly. It finished at 20:03 after the near-duplicate wave resumed on a raised budget, and recall never returned a retired copy.

    2. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026, 14:58 Running

      Marker chains, when the kept copy gets retired

      We found 527 duplicate markers that had quietly switched off because the copy they pointed to was later retired. The fix is installed, the repair has run, and the first protected nightly run is next.

    3. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026, 14:07 Finished

      The incoming filter, from shadow to on

      After 24 hours in shadow mode we read all 116 would-be drops by hand and found no durable facts. We then switched the filter on, and its first real drop was written to an owner-only recovery copy before being dropped.

    4. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026, 01:00 Finished

      Memory rarely reaches the agents that need it

      On lane hosts, recall delivered something on only 5 to 10% of prompts, and almost nothing for Grok. The facts were in memory, but queries built from the raw prompt missed them.

    5. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026 Finished

      The CLM side test, and why we stay on JEV

      We tested whether CLM, an 8B model run on one of our own studios, could pick recall context instead of JEV. It could not; it found the needed rows far less often and was fast enough only with a precomputed cache.

    6. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026 Finished

      Grok memory hooks, an A/B test

      In 96 headless Grok runs, a memory-search tool got the fewest knowledge questions wrong and answered about 3× cheaper and 4× faster. Injecting recall into the prompt made Grok worse.

    7. STARDATE 2026.268 25 September 2026 Finished

      The judge kit and its two independent reviews

      We added seven quality items around our agents. Two independent code reviews found real defects, and every high and medium finding is now fixed.

  2. Stardate 2026.267 24 September 2026

    1. STARDATE 2026.267 24 September 2026 Finished

      The recall budget jam and its fix

      Recall fell back to its old behaviour on 1,539 of 1,685 live attempts because its budget ledger was full of phantom charges, while real spend was about $0.0014. We traced the cause and fixed the accounting.
